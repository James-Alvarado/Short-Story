### The Last Train

Every night at exactly 11:47, Daniel stood on the same subway platform and waited for the last train home.

One rainy Thursday, something strange happened.

The station was completely empty, except for an old man sitting on the bench across from him. Daniel had never seen him before.

“You’re late,” the old man said.

Daniel looked at the clock. **11:47.**

“I’m always here at this time.”

The old man smiled. “Not tonight.”

Before Daniel could ask what he meant, the lights flickered. The train arrived without making a sound.

The doors opened.

Daniel stepped inside.

The moment he looked back, the old man was gone.

There was only a small envelope lying on the bench.

His name was written on it.

Inside was a photograph of Daniel as a little boy, standing beside his father.

On the back, there were six words:

**“You still have time to say goodbye.”**

Daniel stared at the photograph.

His father had died ten years ago.

For the first time in years, Daniel picked up his phone and called his mother.

“Mom,” he whispered when she answered, “tell me about Dad.”

Outside the window, the city disappeared into the darkness.

And somewhere behind him, the old man smiled.
